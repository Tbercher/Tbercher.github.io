ABOUT TAYLOR

Born on November 5, 2004, in the dynamic city of Detroit, Michigan, Taylor Elizabeth White possesses an undeniable magnetism and a lifelong passion for the camera. Early Beginnings From her earliest days in Detroit, Taylor’s affinity for modeling was unmistakable. While most children were just finding their footing, she was already captivated by the lens, displaying a natural grace and presence that hinted at her future calling. Her journey in front of the camera truly began at birth, as she instinctively turned everyday moments into her first editorial shoots, setting the stage for a dedicated pursuit of fashion and visual expression. A Lifelong Passion Driven by an innate creative spirit, Taylor has spent her life intuitively refining her craft. Her Midwestern roots bring a grounded, relatable authenticity to her work, while her lifelong dedication to the art of modeling ensures a level of poise and professionalism that is rarely seen in someone her age. For Taylor Elizabeth, modeling isn't just a career ambition—it's a fundamental part of her identity.
