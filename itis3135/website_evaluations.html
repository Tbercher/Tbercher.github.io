Website Evaulation (CRAP Principles)

Airbnb

  • Contrast: The website uses a simple but effective contrast of black text on a white background, The primary font for listings helps distigniush between the names of listings themselves and corresponding information with there being a grey subtext beneath it describing more details such as the the price per nights and the rating.
  • Repetition: The website uses a consistent card like layout with each listing being card. This card includes an image of the listing, its price per nights, and its corresponding rating overall.
  • Alignment: The website search bars are perfectly centered with the listings card like presentation. Making the large amount of information being displayed manageable.
  • Proximity: All information is grouped together within the card like interface when searching for a listing in an area. Becoming more fleshed out upon selecting a card for futher viewing.

Lings Cars

  • Contrast: Almost a complete and utter failure. The websites white and neon text paired with a neon or white background, along with its floral backdrop makes it difficult focus on any information the website has.
  • Repetition: A blatent lack of consistency throughut the website. Fonts throughout the website use different colors and backdrops, making the website a mess visually increase the difficulty of the websites readability.
  • Alignment: The alignment of the website itself is strucutred fairly well with everything being placed in areas that make sense. With all respective text and images being purposefuly alligned accordingly.
  • Proximity All images and ideas throughout the website are located within a close proximity to each other with image having corrospending information being right below it.
